MedVault

Privacy Policy

MAB Developer built the MedVault app. This Privacy Policy governs the use of the mobile application (the "MedVault") created for healthcare professionals to manage patient logs locally. The Application allows doctors to record and store patient data, prescriptions, and visit dates.

By installing and using the Application, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.


1. Information Collection and Storage Architecture

Patient and Clinical Data (Strictly Local) The Application is built on a local-only data architecture. All patient records, clinical data, prescriptions, and visit dates entered into the application are stored exclusively within the internal storage of your physical mobile device.

    * We do not maintain external cloud servers.
    * We do not collect, read, transmit, backup, or have access to any patient information you input.
    * No patient data is shared with or accessible by any third party.


Information Collected Automatically by Third Parties (Advertising) While the Application does not collect or transmit clinical data, it contains third-party software code to serve advertisements via Google AdMob. To facilitate ad serving, Google AdMob may automatically collect certain device-level information, includings:

    * Your mobile device's advertising identifier (such as the Android Advertising ID / GAID).
    * Internet Protocol (IP) address.
    * Device model, manufacturer, and operating system version.
    * Technical diagnostics and basic usage statistics regarding your interactions with the advertisements.


2. Third-Party Services & Advertising

We integrate Google AdMob to deliver advertisements within the Application. Google AdMob operates independently and may utilize tracking technologies to serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements based on your interests or device parameters.

Critical Safeguard: No patient records, clinical logs, prescriptions, or visit histories entered into the Application are ever shared with, or accessible by, Google AdMob or any external advertising network. Your clinical records are strictly isolated from the advertising framework.

For comprehensive details on how Google processes and protects advertising data, please review the [Google Advertising Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).


3. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We maintain a strict policy against selling, renting, or disclosing your patients' medical records. Because your data remains localized entirely on your physical hardware, it is structurally impossible for us to share it.

Anonymized device metrics and mobile advertising identifiers are shared automatically with Google AdMob to process ad placements. We do not link, combine, or cross-reference your patients' local clinical profiles with these third-party advertising identifiers.


4. Data Retention, Backup, and Deletion

  • Clinical Records: All patient profiles and clinical logs remain on your mobile device as long as the Application is installed. Because we do not back up your data to a cloud, **uninstalling the Application or wiping your device's cache will permanently delete all stored patient records, prescriptions, and visit histories.** This data cannot be recovered by the developer.
  • AdMob Logs: Retention periods for automatically collected device identifiers and ad metrics are managed directly by Google in accordance with their corporate retention frameworks.

    • 5. Regulatory Compliance & Security Disclaimer

    Compliance (HIPAA, GDPR, and Medical Records Privacy):

    Because all data is hosted locally on your device, you (the healthcare provider) retain sole custody and physical control of the Protected Health Information (PHI) or personal data. You are independently responsible for maintaining the physical security of your mobile device, setting strong device passwords, enabling biometric locks, and ensuring your use of the Application aligns with your local jurisdiction's medical record-keeping laws and patient privacy regulations.

    Security Measures:

    The Application relies on the underlying sandbox security model of your mobile operating system to isolate your stored database from other applications on your device.


    6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

    We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time to adapt to technical changes or evolving app store specifications. You are advised to review this policy periodically for any updates. Changes are effective immediately upon being posted within the application settings or on the store listing.
